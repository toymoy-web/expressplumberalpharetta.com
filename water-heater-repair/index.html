Signs You Need A Repair

Common Water Heater Problems In Alpharetta

No Hot Water

Often a failed heating element or thermocouple, not always a full replacement.

Rusty Or Discolored Water

Usually a sign of internal tank corrosion, especially in older units.

Popping Or Rumbling Noises

Sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank, common with Alpharetta's water mineral content.

Leaking At The Base

Can be a loose fitting or a failing tank; a technician needs to check which before you decide on repair vs. replacement.

Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Most Alpharetta homes built since the 1990s run standard 40 to 50 gallon gas or electric tank units, though tankless adoption has grown in newer subdivisions along the GA-400 corridor.

Georgia's water hardness varies by supplier, but sediment buildup is still one of the most common reasons a tank unit starts rumbling or losing capacity over a few years. A technician can check this during a standard repair visit, and a periodic flush can often push that timeline back.

If your unit is still under a manufacturer warranty, keep the paperwork nearby. We'll note the make, model, and serial number on our invoice so you have what you need if you file a separate manufacturer claim. Most standard tank warranties run 6 to 10 years on the tank itself, shorter on parts and labor, and a technician can help you read the data plate if you're not sure what you have.

Common Questions

Is it worth repairing an old water heater?

It depends on the failure and the unit's age. A technician can tell you honestly whether repair or replacement makes more sense for your specific unit.

Do you work on tankless units?

Yes, tankless repair and descaling is a routine part of our work in Alpharetta's newer homes.

What if the repair doesn't fix it?

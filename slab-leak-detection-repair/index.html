Signs Of A Slab Leak

What Brings Alpharetta Homeowners To This Page

Warm Spot On The Floor

A hot water line leak under the slab often shows up as an unexplained warm patch.

Sound Of Running Water With Nothing On

Water audible through the floor with all fixtures off is a common slab leak sign.

Unexplained Water Bill Increase

A slab leak can run steadily without any visible sign indoors.

Cracking Or Uneven Flooring

In more advanced cases, sustained water under a slab can affect the flooring above it.

Alpharetta's red clay soil shifts with moisture and can stress a slab and the pipes beneath it over time, which is a genuine contributing factor in some of the slab leak calls we get here.

We've found that homeowners who catch a slab leak early, from a warm floor spot or a water bill jump, generally see a smaller repair than those who wait until flooring or baseboards are visibly damaged.

FAQs

Common Questions

How is this different from your general Leak Detection service?

General leak detection covers walls, ceilings, and accessible plumbing. This page is specifically for leaks confirmed to be under a concrete slab, which needs different equipment and often a different repair approach.

Will you have to break up my whole floor?

No, we locate the leak precisely first so only the necessary section, if any, needs to be opened.

What is rerouting, and is it better than repairing under the slab?

Rerouting runs a new line above the slab instead of repairing the buried section. It avoids breaking concrete, though whether it's the better option depends on your specific layout.

Can a slab leak cause structural damage?

Left unaddressed long enough, yes, it can affect flooring and subfloor. Catching it early through tracing rather than waiting for visible damage limits that risk.

Is slab leak repair work permitted or inspected?

Yes, repairs follow the Georgia State Minimum Standard Plumbing Code, based on the current International Plumbing Code (IPC) with state amendments, and a permit typically applies when concrete is opened.

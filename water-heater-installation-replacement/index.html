Signs You Need Replacement

When Repair Isn't The Right Call

Age Over 10 Years

Most tank water heaters are built for roughly a 10 to 15 year service life.

Repeated Repairs

If a unit has failed more than once in a short window, replacement is often more cost-effective.

Tank Leaking At The Seams

A leaking tank itself, not just a fitting, generally means replacement rather than repair.

Rising Utility Bills

An aging unit loses efficiency over time even before it fails outright.

Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Alpharetta's newer subdivisions were generally built with standard venting sized for tank units, which is worth checking before switching to tankless for the first time in an existing house.

Households that have added bathrooms or a larger family since the original unit was installed sometimes outgrow their existing tank's recovery rate. Sizing the replacement to current household use, not just matching what's already there, is part of a proper install, and it's a question we ask before quoting a unit size.

We haul away the old unit as part of a standard replacement, rather than leaving that step to you. If you're converting to tankless, we'll flag any gas line or electrical changes the new unit needs before we quote the job.

Tank Vs. Tankless

Which Fits Your Alpharetta Home

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront cost.
  • Simpler installation.
  • Limited hot water supply.
  • Roughly 10 to 15 year lifespan.

Tankless

  • Continuous hot water on demand.
  • Typically lower running cost over time.
  • Compact, wall-mounted footprint.
  • Longer typical service life than tank units.

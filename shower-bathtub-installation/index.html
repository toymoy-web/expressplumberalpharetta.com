Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Tub-to-shower conversions have become a common request in Alpharetta's established neighborhoods as homeowners update older bathrooms built in the 1990s and 2000s.

Newer construction in Alpharetta increasingly favors larger walk-in showers over combination tub-showers, which we account for when quoting a rough-in for a new build or addition.

We coordinate scheduling with your other remodel contractors when a shower or tub installation is part of a larger bathroom project, rather than working in isolation.

Alpharetta's building department requires a permit and inspection for drain relocation work, which we factor into the timeline for any tub-to-shower conversion or new rough-in.

Accessibility-focused conversions, like curbless shower entries, have become a more common request as Alpharetta's original 1990s and early-2000s homeowners plan to stay in place long-term rather than move to a smaller home.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you do the tile and fixture work too, or just plumbing?

We handle the plumbing: drain, supply lines, and valve. For tile and finish work, we can coordinate with your contractor or recommend one we've worked with before.

Can you convert my tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes, this is a common request; it involves relocating the drain and updating the valve, which we scope out during the initial visit.

Do you handle rough-in for a full remodel?

Yes, including new bathroom additions where we rough in supply and drain lines before walls go up.

How long does a typical conversion take?

Most tub-to-shower plumbing work is completed in a day; full remodel rough-ins depend on the overall project timeline.

Can you add a second showerhead or a handheld sprayer during installation?

Yes, that's a common add-on during a new install or conversion; we'll price it as part of the same visit rather than a separate call, which usually saves you money overall.

Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Municipal water in the Alpharetta area, like most treated supplies, uses chlorine for disinfection, which is the most common taste and odor complaint we hear before installing carbon-based filtration.

Newer Alpharetta construction sometimes includes plumbing already roughed in for a whole-home filtration system, which can simplify installation compared to retrofitting an older home.

We're not able to make specific health claims about filtration removing particular contaminants without water testing first, and we'd rather test than guess at what your water actually needs.

Households with young children or anyone with a compromised immune system sometimes want a more thorough filtration setup; we can discuss what's realistic without overselling a system beyond what your water actually requires or needs to address.

Filter cartridges are a routine, ongoing cost of any filtration system, and we're upfront about that recurring expense when we quote a system rather than only mentioning the upfront install price.

FAQs

Common Questions

What's the difference between this and a water softener?

Filtration addresses taste, odor, chlorine, and sediment. A softener addresses mineral hardness specifically. See our Water Softener page for that separate function; some households want and benefit from both installed together.

Do I need a whole-home system or just one at the kitchen sink?

It depends on your goal. If it's just better-tasting drinking water, a point-of-use system is usually enough and costs less.

How often do filters need to be changed?

It varies by system and household water use; we'll give you a specific interval for whatever system you choose.

Can filtration remove everything from my water?

No single system addresses everything. We're honest about what a given filtration type does and doesn't handle for your specific concern.

Do you install systems for a rental property or is this only for homeowners?

We install for both. Landlords sometimes add a basic sediment or carbon system between tenants; we can quote whichever scope fits the property and its specific needs.

Does filtration installation require a permit?

Whole-home systems tied into the main supply line are typically installed to the Georgia State Minimum Standard Plumbing Code, based on the current International Plumbing Code (IPC); simple point-of-use systems usually don't require a separate permit.

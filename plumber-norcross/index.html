Norcross Background

The Story Behind Norcross

Norcross was chartered in 1870 around a new rail line, making it Gwinnett County's second-oldest city, and its downtown historic district is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

The original railroad-town layout is still walkable today, with historic homes and storefronts sitting close to decades of later residential growth further from downtown.

Norcross has leaned into that history, with its downtown district drawing steady foot traffic for dining and shopping alongside the surrounding residential neighborhoods that have grown up around it over the decades.

Between the historic downtown, its restaurants, and the surrounding residential growth, Norcross gives us a genuine cross-section of the plumbing issues we see across the wider service area in one single city.

A Norcross homeowner asking about a repair is just as likely to be dealing with 1950s-era pipe as with a system installed in the last decade, which keeps our technicians from assuming anything before they actually look.

Emergency Steps

What To Do About A Burst Pipe In Norcross

Close The Main Shutoff

If you can reach it safely, closing the main valve stops the leak from getting worse while you wait.

Cut Power, Don't Touch Outlets

Switch off the breaker for any wet area instead of touching an outlet or plugged-in device.

Move Valuables Away From The Water

Rugs, furniture, and electronics should be moved clear as soon as it's safe to do so.

Local History

A Quick Look At Norcross

Norcross was chartered in 1870 around a new rail line, making it Gwinnett County's second-oldest city, and its downtown historic district is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

The original railroad-town layout is still walkable today, with historic homes and storefronts sitting close to decades of later residential growth further from downtown.

Norcross has leaned into that history, with its downtown district drawing steady foot traffic for dining and shopping alongside the surrounding residential neighborhoods that have grown up around it over the decades.

Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

Shut Off The Water

Close the main shutoff valve if it's safe and easy to reach, to stop the flow at the source.

Cut Power Near Standing Water

Switch off power at the breaker for any area with water near outlets, rather than touching outlets directly.

Move Valuables Clear

Get furniture and electronics away from the water while you wait for a technician.

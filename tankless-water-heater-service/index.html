FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless unit be descaled?

Roughly once a year for most households, though water hardness and usage affect that timeline.

Is switching from tank to tankless worth it?

It depends on your household's hot water use and how long you plan to stay in the home. We can walk through the tradeoff honestly rather than push one option.

Do you work on all tankless brands?

Yes, including Rinnai, Navien, and Rheem tankless lines common in Alpharetta's newer construction.

Does a tankless unit need a permit to install?

Generally yes, under the Georgia State Minimum Standard Plumbing Code, especially when venting or gas line work is involved.

What's the lifespan difference between tank and tankless?

A tank unit typically lasts 10 to 15 years. A well-maintained tankless unit often lasts 20 years or more, partly why the higher upfront cost evens out over time for some households in the long run.

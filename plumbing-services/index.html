Not An Emergency?

Routine Vs. Emergency Calls

Not everything on this list needs same-hour dispatch. A running toilet or a slow drain can usually wait for a scheduled window, while a burst pipe, a gas smell, or an actively flooding fixture should go straight to our emergency line.

Water heater problems fall in between. No hot water on a weekday morning can usually wait a few hours for a scheduled slot, but a leaking tank that's actively spreading water onto flooring is closer to an emergency and worth calling in as one.

If you're not sure which category your issue falls into, call (770) 214-4545 anyway. We'd rather make that call for you than have you guess wrong in either direction.

FAQs

Questions About Our Services

Do you charge a fee just to come out and look?

Ask about current diagnostic and after-hours rates when you call. Either way, you approve a flat price for the actual repair before any work begins.

Can one technician handle more than one issue in the same visit?

Often yes. If you mention everything that's going on when you call (770) 214-4545, we can plan the visit to cover more than one item.

Do you serve areas outside Alpharetta itself?

Yes. See our full service area for the neighboring communities we cover on daily routes.

How do you decide who to send for a job?

Dispatch matches the technician's experience to what you describe on the phone, so a water heater specialist isn't sent to a drain call and vice versa where that distinction matters.

What if I need more than one service done at once?

Tell us everything during the initial call. We'll scope a single visit to cover related work where it makes sense, rather than scheduling separate trips.

Do I need to be home for the whole visit?

Usually yes for diagnosis and the final walkthrough, though some jobs only need you there at the very start and at the end rather than for the entire time in between.

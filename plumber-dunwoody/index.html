A Look Back

How Dunwoody Got Its Start

Dunwoody's name comes from Major Charles Dunwody, a Confederate Army veteran. An extra letter was added by a misspelling that stuck. The area stayed largely rural until the 1960s.

Perimeter Mall's construction in 1971 reshaped the area into a commercial hub, and residents eventually incorporated their own city in December 2008 after a multi-year campaign.

Today Dunwoody balances that commercial identity around Perimeter Center with established, tree-lined residential neighborhoods that have grown up around it over the decades since incorporation.

Hydro Jetting in Alpharetta, GA

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clear grease, scale, and root intrusion that standard cabling can't fully remove. It's the step up for lines that keep clogging despite regular cleaning.

If a line has never been jetted before and has a long history of slow drainage, it's worth asking whether that's the underlying cause the next time we're out for a standard clog rather than repeating the same temporary fix over and over.

When Jetting Makes Sense

Signs Cabling Alone Won't Be Enough

Recurring Clogs After Cabling

A line that clogs again soon after standard cabling often has buildup along the walls that cabling only punches through.

Grease Buildup In Kitchen Lines

Restaurant-style or heavy kitchen use can coat pipe walls with grease that only high pressure fully removes.

Root Intrusion In Older Lines

Established Alpharetta neighborhoods with mature trees see this in older clay and cast-iron sewer lines.

Slow Drainage Across Multiple Fixtures

Can point to scale or buildup along the main line rather than one isolated clog.

Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Alpharetta's mature tree canopy is a recurring source of root intrusion in older sewer lines, and jetting clears roots more completely than cabling alone in lines with a real infestation.

Homes near Alpharetta's historic core with original clay or cast-iron lines are more likely to benefit from jetting than the PVC common in newer subdivisions, which resists buildup better to begin with.

For a line with a real history of recurring clogs, a one-time jetting plus a camera check is often more cost-effective over a year than repeated cabling visits for the same symptom.

Kitchens in Alpharetta's older restaurants and shared retail spaces are among the more frequent jetting calls we get, since grease accumulation there tends to be heavier than in a typical residential kitchen line over the same period of time.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

It depends on pipe material and condition. We camera-inspect first and won't recommend jetting on pipe that can't handle the pressure safely, and we'll explain the alternative if that's the case for your line.

How is this different from the drain cleaning you do for a basic clog?

Standard cabling or augering clears most everyday clogs. Jetting is for buildup or root intrusion along the pipe walls that cabling alone tends to leave behind, even when the water starts flowing again right away.

Can jetting damage my pipes?

Modern jetting equipment is calibrated to pipe type and condition, which is why the camera inspection happens first.

How often should a problem line be jetted preventively?

It varies by the line's history; we can suggest an interval based on what the camera shows and how often you've had issues.

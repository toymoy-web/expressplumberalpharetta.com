FAQs

Common Questions

Do you offer maintenance agreements for commercial properties?

Yes, ask about scheduled maintenance for properties that want regular checks rather than waiting for something to break.

Can you work outside business hours?

Often yes, especially for retail or office spaces where daytime access is disruptive. We'll coordinate scheduling with you.

Do you handle grease trap maintenance?

Yes, for food service and retail properties with grease trap needs.

Is commercial work priced differently than residential?

Scope and fixture count drive pricing more than a residential-vs-commercial label. We quote based on the actual job.

Do you provide documentation for property management or ownership records?

Yes, we can provide invoices and service notes formatted for a property manager's maintenance files or ownership records, ready to file without any extra formatting needed on your end.

Can you set up a recurring maintenance schedule?

Yes, we can set up quarterly or annual visits tailored to your property type, so small issues get caught before they become a larger repair or a business disruption that costs you more in the long run overall.

Do you carry the insurance a commercial property typically requires from a vendor?

Yes, we can provide certificates of insurance for property managers or ownership groups that require them before work begins on-site, so there's no delay getting started.

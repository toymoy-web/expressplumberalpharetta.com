Signs Of A Softener Issue

Common Calls We Get

Water Feels Hard Again

Usually a depleted resin bed or a salt/regeneration issue in an existing system.

Scale Buildup On Fixtures

A sign the softener isn't treating water effectively, or that there's no softener installed at all yet.

System Not Regenerating

Can be a timer, valve, or control head issue rather than a resin problem.

Considering A First Softener

Homeowners dealing with scale and spotting for the first time and weighing whether a softener is worth it.

Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Water hardness in Alpharetta varies by supplier and source, but scale buildup is a common enough complaint that a softener is a routine, not unusual, request here.

Newer Alpharetta homes with tankless water heaters benefit particularly from a working softener, since scale buildup inside a tankless unit is one of the more common reasons for reduced efficiency.

Households on well water, less common in Alpharetta but present in some outlying areas, often have different hardness and mineral profiles than municipal customers, worth testing separately.

A softener that's undersized for a household's actual water use tends to regenerate too often and wear out faster, which is why we size based on real usage rather than just square footage or bedroom count alone.

FAQs

Common Questions

How do I know if I need a water softener?

Signs include scale on fixtures, spotting on dishes and glassware, and appliances failing earlier than expected. A quick hardness test can confirm it before you spend money on a system you may not need.

How long do water softener systems last?

Typically 10 to 15 years for the control valve, though resin may need replacement sooner depending on use.

Will a softener fix all my water quality issues?

It addresses hardness specifically. For taste, odor, or contaminant concerns, see our Water Filtration Installation page, which covers a different function entirely and is often paired with softening.

Do you service systems you didn't originally install?

Yes, we service most residential softener brands regardless of who installed them.

How much maintenance does a softener actually need?

Mostly keeping the salt tank filled and an occasional check of the resin bed. We can show you what to watch for during installation or a service visit, so there aren't any surprises.

Is a water softener installation permitted under Georgia code?

A whole-home softener tied into the main line is typically installed to the Georgia State Minimum Standard Plumbing Code, based on the current International Plumbing Code (IPC) with state amendments.

A Bit Of Background

Roswell's Roots

Roswell dates back to 1854, founded by Roswell King, who built a cotton mill along Vickery Creek and drew workers and planters to the growing town.

That mill-town core, now a well-preserved historic district, sits alongside decades of later suburban growth that make up most of the city's current housing stock, so build year varies more block by block than in some neighboring cities.

Roswell today is one of the larger cities in North Fulton, and the historic district remains a genuine point of local pride distinct from the newer subdivisions surrounding it.

For a technician, that history translates into a practical difference on a service call: a home near the mill-era core is more likely to need a repair approach suited to older pipe, while a house in a newer subdivision calls for standard modern parts.

What To Do About A Burst Pipe In Roswell

Find And Close The Shutoff

Every home has a main water valve, usually near where the line enters the house. Close it if you can reach it safely.

Don't Touch Wet Outlets

If water is near an outlet or plugged-in appliance, cut power at the breaker instead of touching the outlet itself.

Protect What You Can

Get furniture and valuables out of the affected area before it gets worse.

Common Disposal Problems

What We Fix Most Often

Jammed Or Humming Disposal

The motor runs but the blades are stuck. Usually clearable without replacing the unit.

Leaking At The Sink Flange

A worn seal at the top mount is a common, moderate-cost fix.

Unit Won't Turn On At All

Can be a tripped reset button, a wiring issue, or a failed motor, which changes the fix.

Persistent Odor

Usually addressed with cleaning, but a lingering smell after cleaning can indicate a drain-side issue worth checking.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is it safe to reach in and clear a jam myself?

We'd rather you not put a hand near the blades. Most jams can be cleared safely from outside the unit using the reset button and an Allen wrench. Call us if you're not sure how or if that doesn't work.

How long do garbage disposals usually last?

Typically 8 to 12 years with normal household use, though usage habits and what regularly goes down the drain affect that range.

Can you install a disposal where there wasn't one before?

Yes, as long as the sink and under-cabinet plumbing and wiring can accommodate it. We'll check that during the visit and quote accordingly before starting any work.

Why does my disposal smell even after I clean it?

A lingering smell after cleaning sometimes points to a P-trap or drain-side issue rather than the disposal itself, worth a quick check rather than repeated cleaning attempts that won't solve the actual cause.

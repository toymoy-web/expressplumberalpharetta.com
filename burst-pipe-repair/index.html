Common Causes

Why Pipes Burst In Alpharetta Homes

Sudden Cold Snaps

A rare deep freeze can catch exposed or poorly insulated pipe runs off guard, even in a generally mild climate.

Aging Galvanized Pipe

Older supply lines near Alpharetta's historic core corrode from the inside and weaken over time.

High Water Pressure

Pressure well above normal residential range puts ongoing stress on fittings and joints.

Ground Movement

Alpharetta's red clay soil shifts with moisture and can stress buried lines over years.

Before We Arrive

What To Do While You Wait

Shut Off The Main Valve

Close the main water shutoff if it's safe and easy to reach, to stop the flow at the source.

Cut Power Near Standing Water

Switch off power at the breaker for any area with water near outlets or appliances, rather than touching outlets directly.

Move Valuables Clear

Get furniture, electronics, and rugs away from the water while you wait.

Call Our Emergency Line

Our emergency line is answered 24/7 for exactly this situation.

Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Alpharetta doesn't see hard freezes often, which means exposed pipe runs are sometimes under-insulated compared to colder climates, so a rare cold snap can catch a home off guard.

Homes built before the 1990s near Alpharetta's historic core more often have galvanized supply lines, which corrode from the inside and are more prone to failure than the PEX and copper common in newer construction.

If a burst pipe has already caused standing water or soaked flooring by the time we arrive, we'll flag that for our restoration line so drying starts as soon as possible alongside the repair.

A pipe that's already burst once is worth a broader look, since the same conditions, age, pressure, or a cold snap, can affect nearby sections of the same run.

FAQs

Common Questions

What should I do the moment I notice a burst pipe?

Shut off the main water valve if it's safe to reach, then call our emergency line. See the checklist above for more.

Is this covered by homeowner's insurance?

Often, but coverage varies by policy. We can document the damage for your claim; we aren't a party to your policy.

Do you charge more for emergency calls?

Ask about current after-hours rates when you call. Either way, you approve a flat price before any repair work begins.

Will you also handle water cleanup?

If there's already standing water or soaked flooring, that cleanup routes to our dedicated water damage restoration line, separate from the pipe repair itself.

Does the repair follow a specific code?

Yes. Pipe repairs and replacements are done to the Georgia State Minimum Standard Plumbing Code, based on the current International Plumbing Code with state amendments.

Before We Arrive

What To Do While You Wait

Don't Bypass A Failed Device

Never remove or bypass a backflow preventer that fails testing; it exists to protect drinking water from contamination.

Report Suspected Contamination

If you notice unusual taste, color, or odor in tap water after irrigation or other cross-connection use, stop using it and call us or your water utility.

Keep Test Records

Hold onto your annual test certificate; many jurisdictions and insurers require proof of current testing.

Schedule Testing Before The Deadline

Don't wait until a compliance deadline is imminent; device issues found during testing sometimes need parts we have to order.

Local Factors

What This Looks Like In Alpharetta

Alpharetta's water provider requirements for backflow testing apply to both residential irrigation systems and commercial properties, and we can help confirm which applies to your address.

Newer Alpharetta subdivisions with in-ground irrigation almost always have a backflow preventer already installed, which still needs annual testing even if it's never given you a problem.

Commercial properties along Alpharetta's retail and office corridors typically have more frequent inspection requirements than residential customers, which we can help you track.

Newer construction along the GA-400 corridor sometimes already meets current backflow prevention code, but retrofitted irrigation systems in older properties are the calls where we most often find a device missing or overdue for testing.

We keep a record of testing dates for our repeat customers, so we can send a reminder before the next annual requirement comes due rather than leaving it entirely up to you to track.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do I actually need this for a residential irrigation system?

Many municipalities and water providers require it, especially with an irrigation system connected to the potable supply. We can help confirm your specific requirement.

How often does testing need to happen?

Typically annually, though your specific requirement depends on your water provider and local code.

What happens if my device fails testing?

We'll quote a repair or replacement, then retest to confirm it passes before certifying it.

Do you handle commercial backflow testing too?

Yes, we test and service both residential irrigation devices and commercial backflow assemblies.
